A Introduction to Soccer


basic rules

  • No hands allowed. Only the goal keeper can usee their hands
  • No pushing,tripping and grabbing of other players, these are fouls
  • Yellow cards are warnings for fouls, time wasting and arguing,Red cards are earned after 2 yellow cards, red cards mean 1 player is ejected from the game and cannot play in the remaining matches.

How to play

  • Soccer is played with 2 teams of 11 players
  • The way is you win is for your team to score the ball into your opponets goal, whoever has the mos goals wins

Duration

  • A soccer game lasts 90 mintues with 45 mintue matches and a 15 mintue break
